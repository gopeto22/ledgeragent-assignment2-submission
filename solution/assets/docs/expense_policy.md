# Expense Policy

## Meals and client entertainment

- UK dinner cap: 75 GBP per attendee.
- EU dinner cap: 90 EUR per attendee.
- US dinner cap: 85 USD per attendee.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable unless the meal is documented as client entertainment; in that case one drink per attendee is allowed.

## Hotels

- Standard hotel cap: 240 USD per night.
- High-cost city cap: 275 USD per night in New York City, San Francisco, and London.
- Exceptions above the cap require director pre-approval before booking.

## Ground transport

- Taxi or ride-share after 22:00 is reimbursable when public transport is impractical.
- A receipt is required whenever a receipt is available from the provider.
- If a digital receipt cannot be recovered, the employee must include a written explanation and the trip reason.

## Contractors and offsites

- Contractors may claim approved travel for company offsites only when the trip was pre-approved in writing by the sponsoring manager.
- Approved contractor travel uses the same caps as employees.

## Missing receipts

- Receipts are required for any single charge above 25 in local currency.
- Charges at or below 25 may use an attestation instead of a receipt, up to two times per month.
