# Support and Service Credits Policy

## Response times

- Starter: 1 business day.
- Pro: 8 business hours.
- Enterprise: 4 hours.
- Approved pilot programs may have written overrides recorded in the internal knowledge base.

## Availability commitments

- Pro availability commitment: 99.5% monthly uptime.
- Enterprise availability commitment: 99.9% monthly uptime.

## Service credits

### Pro

- Below 99.5% uptime: 10% monthly service credit.
- Below 99.0% uptime: 25% monthly service credit.

### Enterprise

- Below 99.9% uptime: 10% monthly service credit.
- Below 99.5% uptime: 25% monthly service credit.
- Below 99.0% uptime: 50% monthly service credit.

## Credit calculation

- Credits are calculated against the affected service's monthly subscription fee.
- Credits are capped at one month of subscription fees.
